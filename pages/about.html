LinkedIn About

I'm a Principal Software Engineer specializing in AI-assisted diagnostics, secure SDLC automation, global lab automation systems, and large-scale IoT platforms. My career has centered on building high-impact engineering tools and automation frameworks that simplify complexity, eliminate manual effort, and enable teams to operate at scale.

At AMD and Dell, I've architected lab automation frameworks, validation imaging systems, and AI-driven diagnostic and threat-modeling prototypes that modernize engineering workflows. I've also led security compliance efforts across the SDLC, ensuring engineering teams consistently met corporate and regulatory standards through structured processes, live threat-modeling sessions, CVSS scoring, mitigation planning, and automated reporting systems.

Earlier in my career, I invented some of the earliest IoT monitoring platforms, including Watchport Manager and SensorServ - systems that delivered real-time environmental and camera-based monitoring, remote HTTP/HTTPS dashboards, unattended Windows Services, and proactive alerting long before IoT became an industry term. I've also contributed engineering leadership to multi-million-dollar deployments across major customers such as Optimal Robotics, QuikTrip, and NCR.

Across every role, my focus remains the same: design systems that are maintainable, scalable, secure, and capable of supporting thousands of users or devices. I thrive at the intersection of architecture, automation, AI, and security-building platforms that elevate engineering organizations and reduce operational friction.
